# **Voizzy MVP Master Plan**

## **Vision**

Empower independent professionals and small businesses to effortlessly collect and showcase authentic text testimonials, building trust and growing their business through social proof.

## **Mission**

Voizzy removes friction from gathering social proof by providing a clean, easy-to-use platform for requesting, approving, and displaying client testimonials.

## **Target User**

* Freelancers

* Independent service providers

* Small business owners

## **MVP Focus**

* Lean, web-first experience.

* Text testimonials only (audio/video planned for future).

* Voizzy-branded testimonial request form (no customization in MVP).

* "Wall of Love" embeddable widget.

* Dashboard for viewing/approving/deleting testimonials.

* Lightweight admin panel for user management.

* Email/password signup and login (Supabase Auth).
* Google SSO Integration: signup and login.

## **Pricing Tiers**

* **Free Plan**

  * Collect up to 3 testimonials

  * Display testimonials on Wall of Love widget

  * Dashboard to track, approve, and delete testimonials

  * No form customization

  * No AI features

* **Basic Plan – $19/month**

  * Unlimited testimonials

  * Everything in Free Plan

  * No form customization

  * No AI features

* **Premium Plan – $49/month**

  * Everything in Basic Plan

  * AI testimonial summarization

  * Customizable testimonial form (Color \+ Logo)
